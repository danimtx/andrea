# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML5, modern CSS3, vanilla JavaScript. Zero build-step dependencies, perfectly optimized for direct deployment on GitHub Pages and Vercel.

## Users

- Recipient: Andrea, accessing via a personalized mobile or desktop web link sent by the user.
- Author / Presenter: The user asking Andrea to become his girlfriend, who will customize specific personal details (photos, location, date, phone number).

## Product Purpose

An intimate, poetic, unforgettable interactive proposal experience designed specifically for Andrea. The experience takes her through a progression of emotional chapters, captivating celestial visuals, and personal memories before presenting the pivotal question ("¿Quieres ser mi novia?"), accompanied by a playful elusive "No" button, an ecstatic celebration when she answers "Sí", and a reveal of a secret date invitation.

## Positioning

Unlike cliché, generic proposal templates, this is a bespoke high-craft digital experience built around a "Velvet Midnight & Constellation" aesthetic, physical-like microinteractions, heartfelt pacing, and refined interactive storytelling.

## Operating Context

- Viewed primarily on a smartphone (iOS or Android browser) or laptop/desktop browser via a direct link.
- Smooth performance, 60fps animations, touch-friendly hit areas, and responsive elegance are essential.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities:
  - Multi-chapter story progression with fluid transitions and romantic prose dedicated to Andrea.
  - Interactive background: dynamic starlight canvas with glowing celestial particles and constellation lines.
  - Ambient background music player with play/pause and gentle volume ramping.
  - Polaroid memory frames with realistic tilt and easy image replacement slots.
  - Elusive "No" button that teleports/evades touch/mouse with playful escalating messages ("¿Segura?", "¡Piénsalo bien!", "¡Ese botón tiene cosquillas!").
  - Climax celebration: confetti/firework particle explosion on "Sí".
  - Interactive wax-sealed letter unfolding the secret date invitation with customizable date/time/location.
  - Direct WhatsApp callback button ("¡Dije que sí! ❤️").
- Constraints:
  - Must run standalone without npm/node runtime on the client (pure static files).
  - Clear, prominent comments in HTML/JS for easy editing of date, location, and phone number.

## Brand Commitments

- Dedicated to Andrea.
- Aesthetic commitment: "Noche Estrellada (Velvet Midnight)" — deep midnight blue, royal velvet violet, starlight gold accents, luminous white typography.
- Tone: Deeply affectionate, sincere, elegant, playful, never tacky.

## Evidence on Hand

- Recipient name: ANDREA.
- Confirmed flow and elements: story chapters, background music player, polaroid memory slots, elusive "No" button with playful prompts, wax seal letter reveal, secret date card, WhatsApp response button.
- User customization placeholders provided for personal photos, date/location text, and WhatsApp number.

## Product Principles

1. Intimacy over spectacle: every animation and phrase must feel genuine, tender, and deeply thoughtful.
2. Fluid, tactile delight: buttons, cards, wax seals, and starlight feel physically responsive and smooth.
3. Inevitable, joyful romance: the interaction guides playfully to the celebratory "Sí".
4. Zero friction deployment: clean single-directory static web app ready for GitHub and Vercel.
